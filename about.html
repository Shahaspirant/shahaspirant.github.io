OUR STORY

Built on Trust Since 1992

Welcome to Shahzad Motors, a trusted name in the automobile business.

The business is owned by Mohammad Shahzad and was established in 1992. With decades of experience in the automobile sector, Shahzad Motors aims to provide customers with quality vehicles and a dependable buying experience.

We believe that purchasing a car should be simple, transparent and satisfying. Our focus is on understanding the customer's requirements and helping them choose a car that suits their needs and budget.
